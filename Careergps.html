How the Career Check works

An explainable scoring engine — not a blind quiz. Every recommendation can be traced back to the student's own signals.

🧠

Signal capture

Interests, preferred subjects, work-style indicators, academic comfort and family priorities — captured in 5 guided steps.

⚖

Weighted fit engine

Each career is scored against interest, aptitude, academic fit, demand and feasibility — with auditable weights.

✨

AI-style explanation

Every result explains why a pathway fits, what to watch out for, and the next 30-60-90 day action plan.
